Priority bands — what each band means (per Spring et al., SSVC, WEIS 2020, Table 2), followed by the conditions our scorer uses to land a finding there:

Immediate
Develop and release a fix as quickly as possible, drawing on all available resources.
  • any KEV-listed CVE
  • Critical/High with EPSS ≥ 0.9 on an internet-exposed workload in a prod cluster
  • any Critical on an internet-exposed workload in a prod cluster
  • cluster-scoped findings (e.g. ClusterRole RBAC) cap at Out-of-Band and never reach Immediate
Out-of-Band (SSVC: Out-of-Band)
Develop a fix out-of-band, taking resources from other projects and releasing it as a security patch when it is ready.
  • Critical/High in prod with an active escalation signal (host-escape, RBAC-elevation, or privilege-escalation)
  • EPSS ≥ 0.9 in prod
  • any Critical in prod
  • High on an internet-exposed workload in prod
  • cluster-scoped: any Critical, or High in a prod cluster
Scheduled
Develop a fix within regularly scheduled maintenance, using developer resources as normal.
  • High/Medium with no fix available in a namespace flagged as sensitive
  • no-fix Critical/High where the workload is exposed or has an escalation signal
  • Critical in staging/dev
  • High in prod
  • Medium in prod with an escalation signal
Defer
Do not work on the patch at present.
  • everything else, including no-fix CVEs that are neither exposed nor have an escalation signal
